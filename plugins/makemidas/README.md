# MakeMidas plugin

Generate AI images and videos with your MakeMidas credits from Claude Code,
Cursor and other agents.

- **MCP server** (`mcp.json`): `https://makemidas.com/api/mcp`. Sign in once
  with OAuth; tools to generate images and videos, check results, list
  models and credits, and save results to Notion.
- **Skill** (`skills/makemidas`): drives the [`makemidas` CLI](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@makemidas/cli)
  to generate files on your computer.

Docs: https://makemidas.com/developers
